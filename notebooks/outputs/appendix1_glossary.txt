Appendix 1: Glossary of Abbreviations

  AC1           Gwet's AC1 Agreement Coefficient (chance-corrected agreement for categorical data)
  Adelphi DSP   Adelphi Disease-Specific Programme (European COPD reference cohort)
  API           Application Programming Interface
  BMI           Body Mass Index
  CI            Confidence Interval
  CNMA          Component Network Meta-Analysis
  CONSORT       Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials
  COPD          Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
  COVID-19      Coronavirus Disease 2019
  ECLIPSE       Evaluation of COPD Longitudinally to Identify Predictive Surrogate Endpoints
  ppFEV1        Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second, percentage predicted
  GOLD          Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease
  ICC           Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC[2,1] = two-way random effects, absolute agreement, single rater)
  IQR           Interquartile Range
  LLM           Large Language Model
  LOWESS        Locally Weighted Scatterplot Smoothing
  NIH           National Institutes of Health (United States)
  OLS           Ordinary Least Squares (linear regression)
  PRISMA        Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses
  PROGRESS-Plus Place of Residence, Race/Ethnicity/Culture, Occupation, Gender/Sex, Religion,
                Education, Socioeconomic Status, Social Capital — Plus (other context-specific factors)
  PROSPERO      International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews
  QQ            Quantile-Quantile (plot)
  RAISE         Responsible AI in Evidence Synthesis (joint Cochrane/Campbell/JBI/CEE framework)
  RCT           Randomised Controlled Trial
  SD            Standard Deviation
  SE            Standard Error
  SES           Socioeconomic Status
  SMD           Standardised Mean Difference
  TRIPOD-LLM    Transparent Reporting of a multivariable prediction model for Individual Prognosis
                Or Diagnosis — Large Language Models (reporting extension)
  VA            Veterans Affairs (United States health system)
